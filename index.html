How it works

Four steps to your result

Every tool follows the same simple flow so there's nothing new to learn.

1

Upload your file

Drag and drop or click to upload. Supports .xlsx and .json files.

2

Configure options

Choose columns, row counts, output format — only what's relevant to the tool you're using.

3

Process

Click the action button. Processing happens instantly in your browser session.

4

Download result

Get your result file with a summary of what was done — rows processed, files created, columns matched.

🔒 Your files stay private

Every file you upload is processed entirely within your active browser session using in-memory computation. Nothing is written to disk or sent to any server. All data is automatically discarded the moment you close or refresh the tab. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no storage of your data — ever.
